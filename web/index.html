NYC SURVIVE
Times Square Survival Simulator
DIFFICULTY
HOW TO PLAY
Click a survivor, or drag a box around a few
Click the ground to send them there
Click a resource to gather it
B opens the build menu, 1-6 picks a building
WASD pans, scroll zooms, Esc deselects
Buildings burn materials. Empty pile, they break.
Every third night: scavengers. PATROL near a building stops them.
Win: 15 survivors, average level 8.
