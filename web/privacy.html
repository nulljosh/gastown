Privacy Policy

NYC Survive · Last updated 21 October 2026

NYC Survive collects nothing. Your saves stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network calls.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Game data

Your saved games and settings are stored locally on your own device so the app can restore your progress. This data never leaves your device and is not readable by us. On the iPhone, iPad and Mac apps it is deleted when you delete the app. In the web version it stays in your browser until you clear that site's data.

Network access

The app makes no network calls. Nothing is sent from your device to us or to anyone else, and we run no servers that receive information from the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that involves collecting data would also require a new app release, reviewed by Apple, before it could take effect.
