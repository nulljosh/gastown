# Gastown Money

How Gastown makes money. The company plan is `REVENUE.md` in the Code root; the ledger is `GTM.md`.

## Price and rail

$0.99 upfront on the App Store (ASC 6782618198), iPhone and Mac. Apple keeps 15%, so each sale pays about $0.84. The web game at gastown.jaybulb.com/app stays free. The web is the trial and the store is the checkout.

## What is actually sold

The game: a colony to run, machines that run themselves, and the Steam Clock win. Everything the buyer pays for is ours to sell. There are no ads, no timers and no autoplay.

## Who pays and why

A player who likes a colony game with no ads, no timers and no autoplay, and pays once for the whole game.

## Cost to serve

No paid API. The game runs in the browser and natively with no server. Hosting is Cloudflare, and nothing in the code calls a paid service.
