## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Gastown.

I wanted a survival sim where nothing happens unless I decide it. No idle timers, no autoplay. Gastown drops you in Times Square after the lights go out. You place shelters, assign work, ration food and hold the line at night. Every loss is a decision you made.

Free on web. $0.99 once on the App Store, no ads, no timers, nothing else to buy.
