## NYC Survive

One-liner: Times Square has gone dark. Keep them alive.

Description: A colony sim set in a besieged Times Square. Command survivors, build shelter and defenses, keep them fed, rested and breathing at night. No autopilot, every move is yours. Free on web, $0.99 upfront on the App Store.

Category: Games / Simulation
