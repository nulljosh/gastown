What you do

Raise the Steam Clock

Gather materials and power. Drills mine for you and generators keep them running. Build enough and the clock on Water Street whistles again.

Command

Send the crew

Drag a box around survivors, click a resource, and they work it until you say otherwise.

Build

Four buildings

Shelters add beds. Generators make power. A drill beside a resource mines it and burns power. The Steam Clock wins.

Grow

A crew that grows

A free bed and ten food bring a new survivor every day, up to twenty. More hands, a sooner clock.

Your orders

Nobody moves until you say so

Colonists only move when you tell them to. Pick one, then pick the spot. Machines are the only thing that works alone.

Controls

Touch or keys

Tap a survivor, then the ground. On Mac, WASD pans, B opens the build menu, 1 to 4 pick a building and Space pauses.

The win

The clock whistles

Raise the Steam Clock and Gastown is yours. About fifteen minutes if you build well.

A real run

See it played

Drag a box around survivors, click a resource to gather it, and press B to build.
