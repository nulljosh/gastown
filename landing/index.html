NYC Survive

Lead a small crew, street by street. Send them to work, build machines that run on their own, and raise the Beacon.

The real game, running live. Drag a box around a few survivors, then click a resource.

What you do

Raise the Beacon

Gather materials and power. Drills mine for you and generators keep them running. Build enough and the Beacon goes up.

Command

Send the crew

Drag a box around survivors, click a resource, and they work it until you say otherwise.

Build

Four buildings

Shelters add beds. Generators make power. A drill beside a resource mines it and burns power. The Beacon wins.

Grow

A crew that grows

A free bed and ten food bring a new survivor every day, up to twenty. More hands, faster Beacon.

Your orders

Nobody moves until you say so

Colonists only move when you tell them to. Pick one, then pick the spot. Machines are the only thing that works alone.

Controls

Touch or keys

Tap a survivor, then the ground. On Mac, WASD pans, B opens the build menu, 1 to 4 pick a building and Space pauses.

The win

The Beacon stands

Raise it and Times Square is yours. About fifteen minutes if you build well.

A real run
