Lead a small group of survivors, street by street. Build what they need and get them through the night.

What you do

Hold the block

Buildings burn materials. Survivors get hungry, tired and stressed. Keep the numbers up and the colony holds.

Build

Six kinds of building

Shelters, food stalls, generators, filter stations, subway access and billboards.

Keep alive

Five numbers to watch

Hunger, oxygen, stress, sleep and health. Let one slide and someone pays for it.

Nights

Raids come after dark

Put someone on patrol before the raids hit. Have shelters up and food stocked.

Your orders

Nobody moves until you say so

Colonists only move when you tell them to. Pick one, then pick the spot.

Controls

Touch or keys

Tap a survivor, then the ground. On Mac, WASD pans, B opens the build menu and Space pauses.

The win

Times Square reclaimed

Grow to 15 survivors, average level 8, with one at level 10.

A real run

See it played

Drag a box around survivors, click a resource to gather it, and press B to build.
